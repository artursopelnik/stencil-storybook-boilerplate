## Intent

Use an avatar to represent a person or entity with an image or their initials, for example in navigation bars, comment threads, or user lists. Do NOT use an avatar for decorative illustrations or content images — use a plain image instead.

---

## Guidelines

- **Do** always provide `initials` so a meaningful fallback appears when the image fails to load.
- **Do** provide a descriptive `alt` text for the image.
- **Do** keep initials to one or two characters.
- **Don't** use avatars to display logos or arbitrary icons.
- **Don't** mix shapes within the same list of people.
