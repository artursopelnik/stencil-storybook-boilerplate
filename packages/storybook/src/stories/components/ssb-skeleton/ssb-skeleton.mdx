`ssb-skeleton` renders a pulsing placeholder shape while content is loading. The look follows the Basecoat UI skeleton.

## Table of Contents

- [Intent](#intent)
- [Guidelines](#guidelines)
- [Examples](#examples)
- [Properties](#properties)

---

## Intent

Use a skeleton to reserve space for content that is still loading, mirroring the final layout as closely as possible. Do NOT use a skeleton for indeterminate operations without layout, such as form submissions — use `ssb-spinner` instead.

---

## Guidelines

- **Do** match the skeleton's width, height, and radius to the content it replaces.
- **Do** combine several skeletons to sketch the final layout of the loading view.
- **Do** replace skeletons with real content as soon as it is available.
- **Don't** keep skeletons on screen after loading has failed — show an error state instead.
- **Don't** announce skeletons to assistive technology — they are purely decorative.
