`ssb-scroll-area` constrains its slotted content to a maximum height and shows slim, subtly styled scrollbars in the configured direction.

## Intent

Use a scroll area to keep long secondary content, such as tag lists or logs, within a bounded region of the layout. Do NOT use a scroll area for a page's primary content — let the document scroll naturally instead.

---

## Guidelines

- **Do** set `maxHeight` so the surrounding layout stays stable regardless of content length.
- **Do** use `orientation="horizontal"` for rows of cards or chips that overflow sideways.
- **Do** make sure content is still reachable via keyboard scrolling and focus.
- **Don't** nest scroll areas — inner scrollbars inside scrollbars are hard to use.
- **Don't** hide critical actions below the fold of a small scroll area.
