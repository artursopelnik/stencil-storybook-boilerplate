`ssb-progress` shows how far a determinate operation has advanced as a filled bar. The indicator animates smoothly when the value changes.

## Intent

Use a progress bar to communicate the completion state of a determinate operation such as an upload, download, or multi-step flow. Do NOT use a progress bar for operations of unknown duration — use a spinner or skeleton state instead.

---

## Guidelines

- **Do** provide an accessible name via the `label` prop or the `aria` prop.
- **Do** keep `value` between `0` and `max`; the indicator is clamped either way.
- **Do** update the value in reasonably sized increments so the animation stays smooth.
- **Don't** animate the bar backwards — decreasing progress confuses people.
- **Don't** use a progress bar as a static decoration or rating display.
