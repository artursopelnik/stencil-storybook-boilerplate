`ssb-kbd` renders a keyboard key in a small monospace chip. The look follows the Basecoat UI kbd element.

---

## Intent

Use a kbd element to display keyboard shortcuts or key names inside documentation, tooltips, and menus. Do NOT use it for badges, tags, or other non-keyboard labels — use `ssb-badge` instead.

---

## Guidelines

- **Do** render one key per `ssb-kbd` and join combinations with a plus sign or spacing.
- **Do** use platform-appropriate symbols such as `⌘` on macOS and `Ctrl` on Windows.
- **Don't** use kbd chips as interactive buttons — they are purely presentational.
- **Don't** put whole sentences inside a kbd chip.
