---

## Intent

Use a badge to highlight the status or category of an item, e.g. counts, labels or states. Do NOT use a badge as an interactive element — use `ssb-button` when a click action is required.

---

## Guidelines

- **Do** keep badge text short — one or two words or a count.
- **Do** use the `destructive` variant for error or warning states.
- **Don't** attach click handlers to badges; they are purely presentational.
