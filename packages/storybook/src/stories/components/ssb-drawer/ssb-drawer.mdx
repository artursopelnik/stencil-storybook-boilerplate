`ssb-drawer` is a panel that slides in from an edge of the viewport over a backdrop.

---

## Intent

Use a drawer for supplementary content or actions that slide in on demand — filters, carts, mobile-friendly forms. Do NOT use it for blocking confirmations — use `ssb-alert-dialog` — or for permanent navigation, where `ssb-sidebar` belongs.

---

## Guidelines

- **Do** provide a `drawerTitle` so the dialog has an accessible name.
- **Do** prefer `side="bottom"` on small screens; it matches the familiar sheet pattern.
- **Don't** nest drawers inside drawers.
- **Don't** put critical, irreversible actions solely inside a drawer that can be dismissed by a backdrop click.
