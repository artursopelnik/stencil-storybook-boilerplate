`ssb-spinner` shows an animated loading indicator with a visually hidden label. The look follows the Basecoat UI spinner.

## Intent

Use a spinner to indicate an in-progress operation without a known layout, such as submitting a form or fetching data behind a button. Do NOT use a spinner where the final layout is known — use `ssb-skeleton` placeholders instead.

---

## Guidelines

- **Do** provide a meaningful `label` describing what is loading, e.g. "Saving…".
- **Do** use the `sm` size inside buttons and inline contexts.
- **Do** remove the spinner as soon as the operation finishes.
- **Don't** show several spinners for the same operation.
- **Don't** use a spinner for long operations with known progress — prefer a progress indicator.
