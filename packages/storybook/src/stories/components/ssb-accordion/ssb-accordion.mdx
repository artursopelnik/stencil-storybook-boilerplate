`ssb-accordion` groups `ssb-accordion-item` children into a vertically stacked set of collapsible sections. By default only one item is open at a time.

## Table of Contents

- [Intent](#intent)
- [Guidelines](#guidelines)
- [Examples](#examples)
- [Properties](#properties)

---

## Intent

Use an accordion to let people progressively disclose longer, secondary content such as FAQs or settings groups. Do NOT use an accordion for critical content people must read — show that content directly instead.

---

## Guidelines

- **Do** keep item headings short and scannable.
- **Do** set `multiple` when sections are independent and people may want to compare them.
- **Do** open the most relevant item by default when one section clearly matters most.
- **Don't** nest accordions inside accordions.
- **Don't** hide required form fields or errors inside collapsed items.
