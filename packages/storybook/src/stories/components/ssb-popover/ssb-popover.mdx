`ssb-popover` toggles a floating panel from a slotted trigger on click. Clicking outside or pressing Escape closes it, and every change emits `ssbOpenChange`.

## Table of Contents

- [Intent](#intent)
- [Guidelines](#guidelines)
- [Examples](#examples)
- [Properties](#properties)

---

## Intent

Use a popover for lightweight, contextual content such as small forms, filters or extra details anchored to a trigger. Do NOT use a popover for short text hints — use `ssb-tooltip` — or for critical decisions, which belong in `ssb-dialog` or `ssb-alert-dialog`.

---

## Guidelines

- **Do** put a focusable element such as `ssb-button` into the `trigger` slot.
- **Do** pick `position` and `align` so the panel stays inside the viewport.
- **Do** listen to `ssbOpenChange` when other UI depends on the popover state.
- **Don't** put destructive confirmation flows inside a popover.
- **Don't** open a popover automatically without a user interaction.
