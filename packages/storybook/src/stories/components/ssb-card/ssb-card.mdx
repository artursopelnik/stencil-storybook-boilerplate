`ssb-card` groups related content in a bordered container with title, description, action, content, and footer areas. The layout follows the Basecoat UI card.

## Table of Contents

- [Intent](#intent)
- [Guidelines](#guidelines)
- [Examples](#examples)

---

## Intent

Use a card to group related information and actions into a self-contained surface, such as a settings section, a summary tile, or a form container. Do NOT use a card as a generic wrapper for whole pages or for single inline elements.

---

## Guidelines

- **Do** provide a concise title in the `card-title` slot and supporting copy in the `card-description` slot.
- **Do** place primary actions in the `footer` slot and secondary icon actions in the `action` slot.
- **Do** keep the content of one card focused on a single topic.
- **Don't** nest cards inside cards.
- **Don't** overload a card with more than one primary action.
